# fxc
Bank of Canada Foreign Exchange Currency Converter

NOTE: these are NOT real-time live exchange rates. They are end-of-day closing exchange rates provided every business day by the Bank of Canada 
https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/daily-exchange-rates/

Use Advanced Options to enter in your own amounts so you can compare and calculate any added fees or charges based on how different another rate is to the closing end-of-day mid-market Bank of Canada foreign exchange rate (which has no fee built-in).
